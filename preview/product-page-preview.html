Why PawVoyage Beds

The Dog Dock is a secure travel bed designed to create a defined space for your dog during car journeys. With ISOFIX anchoring, reinforced safety straps, and a built-in harness clip, it stays firmly positioned on the seat, helping reduce movement, distraction, and unsettled behaviour while travelling.

Convenient Side-Pocket Storage

Keep essentials close at hand, from leads and treats to travel toys. The secure pocket means you won't have to juggle multiple bags while travelling with your dog.

Adjustable Car Seat Belts

Secure the bed to any car seat with adjustable straps. Designed for stability, they keep it anchored, protecting your pet during sudden stops or turns.

Non-Slip Safety Base

The sturdy, anti-slip bottom keeps the bed secure on car seats or smooth floors, preventing sliding during travel and giving your pet a steady place to relax.

Metal ISO Fix Clasps

Metal ISOFIX clasps lock directly into your car's ISOFIX anchors, keeping the Dog Dock firmly secured and reducing unwanted movement while driving.

Removable, Plush Inner Liner

Soft, removable liner that is easy to wash and replace, so your dog always has a clean resting space at home or on the go. Waterproof on one side too.

Built in Safety Clip

Attach your dog's harness to the integrated safety clip for peace of mind on every trip. Prevents jumping out or sudden movement.
