It's not the outfit. It's the presence.

Thin fabric loses its shape after three washes. Our pieces are cut from 450gsm cotton that holds its structure, so the fit you buy is the fit you keep.

More about our fabric

Questions? We've got you.

Our pieces are made to make you feel confident, inside and out.

Find your answer below or send us a message.

Everything is cut oversized and unisex. Take your usual size for a relaxed drop-shoulder fit, or one size down for a closer fit. Each product page has a size chart in centimetres.

Hoodies are 450gsm brushed-back cotton fleece. Tees are 260gsm combed cotton jersey. Both are pre-shrunk.

Orders ship within 1–3 business days. Delivery takes 2–5 business days in the Netherlands and Belgium and 5–10 days across the rest of Europe. Every order includes tracking.

Most drops are limited and are not restocked. Sign up to the newsletter to hear about the next drop first.

Wash inside out at 30°C with similar colours and hang to dry. Avoid the tumble dryer to keep the print sharp.

You can return unworn items in their original packaging within 30 days. We refund once the return has been checked.
