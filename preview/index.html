The coverless difference

A beautiful bed. A simpler routine.

With a traditional duvet, making the bed means turning a cover inside out, finding the corners and working the insert back in. Here, the outer fabric and the filling are one piece — so that step simply isn’t there.

Lay it on the bed, smooth it out, and the room looks finished. On laundry day, the whole duvet is washed together and goes straight back on.

Our difference

A different way to make your bed.

A different way to make your bed.
Duvet + separate cover Coverless duvet
Separate cover required Yes — the insert and cover are bought and fitted separately. No — the outer fabric is part of the duvet.
Making the bed Turn the cover inside out, match the corners, work the insert in and close the fastening. Unfold, spread over the bed and smooth into place.
What gets washed Usually the cover; the insert less often, sometimes needing a large machine or specialist cleaning. The whole duvet, as one piece. Check your machine’s capacity for larger sizes.
Sizes & styling Change the look anytime by swapping covers. Five sizes and ten colors. Changing the look means choosing another duvet.

A coverless duvet suits people who value an easier routine; a separate cover suits people who like to change their look often.
