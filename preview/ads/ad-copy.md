## Campaign setup (starter)
- **Objective:** Sales, with the conversion event set to Purchase. Install the Meta pixel and the Shopify Facebook & Instagram app first.
- **Structure:** 1 campaign (Advantage+ campaign budget) with 1 broad ad set: ages 25–65, Advantage+ audience on, no interest stacking. Add all 9 creatives with copy A–D and let Meta rotate them.
- **Markets to test first (from the earlier research):** US, Australia, Germany. Run Germany with translated copy.
- **Budget:** about $50–100/day to start. Judge after 3–4 days on cost per purchase, then turn off the creatives with the weakest click-through rate and add new hooks.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements. Feed images go to Feed and Explore, the 9:16 versions to Stories and Reels.

## Before you launch
1. **Activate the products** in Shopify (they're still drafts), otherwise the link 404s.
2. **Make sure every claim is true:**
   - The ads say "free express shipping", "30-day free returns" and "lifetime warranty". Your current shipping policy says 8–14 days and your refund policy says the customer pays return shipping, so update the policies or remove those lines.
   - "50% off" needs a real former price. Meta and consumer law require that the item was actually sold at $300.
3. **Don't use the sample reviews in ads.** The reviews on the site are placeholders. Only quote real customer reviews.
4. **Change the "Ends Saturday" copy** after Black Friday.
