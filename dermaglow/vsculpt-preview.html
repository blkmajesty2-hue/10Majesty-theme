Description

A cooling hydrogel mask shaped to wrap from under the chin up to the ears. The gel holds a firming serum against the jawline and neck while the stretch loops give a gentle lifting hold. After one session, the lower face looks tighter, smoother and less puffy.

Key ingredients

Hero ingredient: Opuntia (cactus) extract for cooling and de-puffing.

Supporting actives: Caffeine, Collagen, Peptide complex, Centella Asiatica, Hyaluronic acid.

Each box: 4 gel masks, 9.3g each.

How to use

Cleanse and dry your face. Hook the loops over your ears and smooth the gel under your chin. Relax for 20 to 30 minutes, remove, and massage in the remaining serum. Use 2 to 3 times a week.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it work for men?

Yes. The mask is unisex and the stretch loops fit most face sizes.

How soon will I see results?

Most people notice a cooler, less puffy look after the first session. For a firmer-looking jawline, use it 2 to 3 times a week for 4 weeks. Results vary from person to person.

Is the mask reusable?

Each mask is single-use, because the serum is absorbed during wear. Each box contains 4 masks.

Will it get rid of a double chin?

V-Sculpt is a cosmetic mask. It helps the chin and jawline look firmer, smoother and less puffy. It does not remove fat.

Can I use it on sensitive skin?

It is dermatologist-tested and fragrance-free. Patch-test on your inner arm first and stop using it if irritation occurs.
