# Market Finder: countries where the duvet is NOT copied yet (28 Oct 2026)

Goal: find a country where nobody is selling a copy of the Les Dormeurs / "EasySleep" 2-in-1 coverless duvet yet.
Source: Brandsearch (Meta ads, EU/UK transparency data only). Ad counts are for ads started since 1 May 2026.
Spend figures are Brandsearch **estimates**.

## How the copies were found

Almost every copy uses the same template: product name "EasySleep" (or a renamed twin), "2 free SoftCloud pillowcases",
"40-night trial". Searching those words across all EU/UK ads finds **3,691 ads** (EasySleep) and **4,306 ads** (SoftCloud).
Each country was then checked again with the local word for the product, for example "kołdra 2 w 1" (PL),
"přikrývka 2 v 1" (CZ), "pilotă 2 în 1" (RO), "paplan 2 az 1-ben" (HU), "πάπλωμα 2 σε 1" (GR) and "edredão 2 em 1" (PT).
To make sure a zero was real, I also checked that the same search finds ordinary duvet ads there: HU 160, RO 702 and
GR 251 ordinary duvet ads were found.

## Scorecard: uncopied countries (0–5 × weight, max 70)

| Criterion (weight) | Portugal | Poland | Greece | Hungary | Romania |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proof of demand (×3) | 2 → 6 | 3 → 9 | 1 → 3 | 1 → 3 | 1 → 3 |
| Gap / no copies (×3) | 5 → 15 | 4 → 12 | 5 → 15 | 5 → 15 | 5 → 15 |
| Price room (×2) | 3 → 6 | 3 → 6 | 2 → 4 | 2 → 4 | 1 → 2 |
| Effort to launch (×2) | 3 → 6 | 1 → 2 | 1 → 2 | 1 → 2 | 1 → 2 |
| Delivery and risk (×2) | 3 → 6 | 3 → 6 | 2 → 4 | 2 → 4 | 2 → 4 |
| Audience fit (×1) | 3 → 3 | 3 → 3 | 2 → 2 | 2 → 2 | 2 → 2 |
| Ad cost and saturation (×1) | 5 → 5 | 4 → 4 | 5 → 5 | 5 → 5 | 5 → 5 |
| **Total** | **47** | **42** | **35** | **35** | **33** |

Why:

- **Portugal.** There is no direct proof, but next-door **Spain is proven**: cosalia-home.com has advertised there since 30 Jun and still runs 40 active ads.
  Bedding sells through Meta in Portugal (momo-cotton.pt has 22 active ads, and naideva.com advertises duvets at €79–109).
  Portugal is **already in your Shopify "Europe" market** (EUR, with a shipping zone). It is under the same EU VAT/IOSS rules as the rest. It needs Portuguese text.
- **Poland.** Two copy stores started in the last 10 days. That is a signal that someone believes in it, but it means the window is closing.
  Poland is a large (37M people), cold market. It is not in your Shopify markets yet, and it needs PLN, Polish text and local payments (BLIK / Przelewy24).
- **Greece, Hungary, Romania.** Completely open, but there is no proof that anyone buys this product there. Cash-on-delivery habits are common.
  Romanian duvet ads sell full sets for 139–190 lei (about €28–38), which is a low price anchor.

## Pick

1. **Portugal first.** No copies, a proven neighbour (Spain), and the market is already set up in your store. It is the one that
   passes the method's rules best (score 47; proof is only indirect, so start with a **small** test budget).
2. **Poland as backup**, and move fast if you choose it. The first copies appeared on 18 and 23 Oct.
3. Greece, Hungary and Romania are "open but unproven". The method says no ads does not mean free demand, so only try them with a tiny test.

## Blind spots

- An untouched market may mean nobody has tried yet, or that people tried and it failed. The Romanian and Greek tests ran 1–2 days with about €0 spend, which is too small to tell.
- Data covers Meta ads in the EU/UK only. TikTok, Google and local marketplaces (OLX, Allegro, eMAG, Skroutz) were not checked.
- The search relies on how copies are named. A copy with a totally different name and wording could be missed.
- The blockers from the first report still apply (the Pleene name on your products, compare-at prices, unknown landed cost).

**English pick:**
- **Ireland** is the practical choice. It uses English, so you can reuse UK-style copy. It is EUR, already in your Shopify Europe market and under EU VAT/IOSS. It has a cold, damp climate, and UK proof carries over because Irish people see the same ads.
- The downside: UK copy ads already reach some Irish users, and the market is small (about 5M people).
- **New Zealand** is the only English market where no copy was found at all. But the tool is blind there (no reach data), delivery from a typical supplier is long and costly, and the market is small. Test it only with a tiny budget.
