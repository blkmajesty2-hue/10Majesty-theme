Frequently asked questions

Before you choose.

Product and shopping information,
in plain English.

Does shapewear permanently change my shape?

Treat this as a shaping garment worn under clothes, not a promise of lasting body changes. Permanent reshaping claims on the source have not been reproduced.

Will it show under clothes?

The product is marketed as seamless. The visible finish depends on fit, color and the clothing worn over it.

Is it suitable after childbirth, a C-section or during menopause?

For postpartum or post-surgical wear, ask your clinician whether this garment is appropriate for you. The source’s medical suitability claims have not been independently verified.

How long can I wear it?

Follow the actual garment’s instructions and choose a comfortable fit. The reference’s wear-duration claims are not verified in this draft.

How should I wash it?

The source recommends hand washing or a gentle machine cycle with mild detergent, followed by air drying. It advises against bleach and fabric softener.

What sizes are available?

The source lists S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL, 4XL, 5XL and 6XL. Check the original size chart before choosing.

How does the cross-band design work?

The overlapping front panels are the distinguishing construction detail. The source presents this as wraparound support for the midsection.

Will the waistband roll down?

The structured high waist is marketed as a stay-in-place design. Fit and movement can affect how a garment wears.

Can I choose different colors in one bundle?

Yes. This draft lets you select the size and color of every brief in the chosen bundle, matching the reference’s variant-based offer.

How do I choose my size?

Measure your waist and compare it with the source size chart. The source recommends the larger size when between sizes.

How long is delivery?

The source lists 7–12 business days for delivery in Italy and tracking after dispatch.

What if I want to return my order?

The source advertises a 30-day money-back guarantee. Review its current return terms before purchasing from the actual store.
