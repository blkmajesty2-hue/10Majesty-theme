Can I wash this duvet in the machine?

Yes. The duvet is machine washable as one piece — there’s no insert to take out and no cover to remove. Follow the care label sewn into the duvet for the temperature and cycle.

Larger sizes need a large-capacity drum, so check your machine before washing a Queen or King.

How fast does it dry?

It’s quick-drying, so you can wash it, dry it and have it back on the bed the same day. Follow the drying instructions on the care label.

Are pillowcases included?

No — each order contains the duvet only.

Which size for my bed?

Choose the size that matches your bed. For more overhang, go one size up.

Frequently asked questions

Do I still need a duvet cover?

No — that’s the whole point. The outer fabric and the filling are a single product, so you use it straight on the bed with nothing to fit.

When it needs a wash, the whole thing goes in the machine, without a cover.

What is it made of?

A breathable outer fabric with a soft filling, made as one piece. You’ll find the exact composition on the care label.

How do I wash and dry it?

Put the whole duvet in the machine and follow the care label. It’s quick-drying, so it can be back on your bed the same day.

Will it fit in my washing machine?

Smaller sizes fit most home machines. For a Queen or King, use a large-capacity drum — if yours is too small, a laundromat machine works well.

Which size should I choose?

Pick the size that matches your bed: Single, Twin, Full, Queen or King. Tap “Which size should I choose?” above the Add to Bag button for exact dimensions.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed in 1–3 business days, then take 5–10 business days to arrive in the US and Canada. Every order is tracked.

Delivery, guarantee and returns

You’re covered by our 90-day money-back comfort guarantee. See our refund and shipping policies for the full conditions.

Any other question? Write to us at support@blkmajesty.com and we’ll be happy to help.
